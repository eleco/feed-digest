A SMALLER DAILY RITUAL

From open tabs
to a clearer head.

01

Tell it what matters.

Start with Basic mode, or explicitly set up a provider using your own API key.

02

Let a scan do the scrolling.

A dedicated, visible X tab samples both feeds. Each feed stops at 100 posts, 90 seconds, or an earlier limit by default. Configure up to 1,000 posts and 100 scroll steps per feed in Settings. Stop whenever you like.

03

Read at your own pace.

Open your digest, give useful feedback, or export Markdown and JSON. Every collected post stays visible, with AI categories and takeaways when available.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your reading habits
are your business.

Posts, preferences and digests live in your browser by default. No analytics or tracking. The optional online reader displays only the collection you explicitly export and upload; it cannot access your X account or AI API keys.

Online AI is optional. Clicking Analyse with AI sends collected text and quoted text to OpenRouter and its model providers, or to OpenAI or Anthropic Claude. Scanning alone makes no AI requests. You can stop analysis or remove your key at any time.

READY FOR A MORE INTENTIONAL FEED?

Make a little room
for the good stuff.

This is a developer preview, installed locally in Chrome.
No Chrome Web Store listing or hosted account is required.

  1. From the project folder, run npm ci && npm run build.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Choose Load unpacked, then select the dist folder.
  4. Sign into X normally, open the extension and choose Scan both feeds.
Limitations & platform rules

English X UI only. Live X selectors and real provider compatibility still require an authorised smoke test. A scan samples rendered posts; it does not read images, full threads or articles. Scheduling is best effort and requires a visible tab unless explicitly enabled to bring it forward.

X’s automation rules prohibit scripting its website and warn of account suspension. DOM access does not establish compliance or account safety. Review X’s current rules before use or release.
